About HandyNaija

How it works

HandyNaija connects you with verified local professionals — electricians, plumbers, tutors, mechanics and more — and keeps the whole job visible from request to completion.

Four steps from problem to done

  1. 1

    Search

    Tell us the service and your state or city. We show providers with real ratings, real reviews and the areas they actually cover.

  2. 2

    Request

    Describe the job in your own words, attach a photo if it helps, and choose a preferred date and time. Nothing is booked until the provider agrees.

  3. 3

    Connect

    The provider accepts, or declines with a reason. Message each other to confirm the price, the arrival time and anything else that matters.

  4. 4

    Complete

    Follow the status as the job progresses, pay once it is done, then leave a review so the next customer knows who to trust.

Provider verification

A verified badge means we have checked the provider's identity and business documents ourselves. It is not something a provider can buy, and it cannot be self-declared.

What we check

  • Government-issued identification
  • Business registration where applicable
  • A utility bill proving the operating address
  • Trade qualifications for specialist work
  • A working phone number confirmed by call

Why it matters

Unverified providers can still use HandyNaija, but their profile is labelled clearly and they appear below verified ones in search results. Verification typically takes two to three working days.

If a provider asks for payment outside the platform before turning up, that is a warning sign. Report it and we will act on it.

Staying safe

Before the visit

  • Agree the price and scope in writing before work starts
  • Keep the conversation inside HandyNaija messages
  • Be present, or leave a trusted adult in charge
  • Ask for identification if you did not book them yourself

After the visit

  • Check the work before the provider leaves
  • Pay only once the job is genuinely finished
  • Leave a review — it protects the next customer
  • Report anything that felt wrong

Common questions

How much does it cost to use HandyNaija?

Creating an account and sending a request is free. Agree the price directly with your provider before the work starts.

How do I choose between providers?

Start with verification, then rating and review count, then whether the provider covers your exact area. Response time matters too — someone who answers quickly is usually someone who turns up.

What if a provider declines my request?

You will see the reason, and you are free to send the request to someone else. A declined request costs you nothing.

Can I cancel a request?

Yes. You can cancel while a request is pending, accepted or scheduled. Once the provider has started the work, get in touch so you can agree how to finish it.

I am a professional. How do I get work?

Register as a provider, complete your profile, list your services with prices, and set the hours you are available. Requests then arrive in your queue.
