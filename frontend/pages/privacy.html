Privacy Policy

We collect the minimum we need to introduce you to a professional and to keep the job safe. This page explains exactly what that means.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, phone number and role.
  • Profile details — for providers, your business name, categories, bio, service areas, experience and verification documents.
  • Request details — the description of the job, location, preferred time, photos you attach and any notes.
  • Messages — the conversation you have with a provider about a job.
  • Reviews — the rating and comment you publish after a completed service.
  • Technical data — your browser type, device type and pages visited, used to keep the service working.

We do not collect your exact home address unless a provider needs it for an accepted job. The area you choose at search time is not shared beyond the providers you contact.

Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account
  • To show you providers who cover your area and service
  • To pass a request to the provider you choose
  • To send the notifications that tell you a job has moved on
  • To investigate reports, disputes and abuse
  • To improve search results and fix problems

Who we share it with

We share your contact details with a provider only after you send them a request, and never with anyone else for marketing. We share aggregated, anonymised totals with nobody — our statistics are counts, not identities.

We may disclose information where Nigerian law requires it, for example to a court or a law enforcement agency acting lawfully.

Data stored on this device

This build keeps your session, your preferences and any half-finished request in your browser's local storage so the site keeps working between page loads. Nothing sensitive is written to your device by a third party. Clearing your browser data signs you out.

Cookies and similar technology

We use local storage rather than advertising cookies. We do not run third-party advertising or tracking pixels. If we introduce analytics later, this page will say so before it goes live.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is open and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can honour reviews and resolve disputes. Records tied to a completed job are kept for the period required by Nigerian consumer protection rules.

Your rights

  • See the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your account and its data
  • Ask us to stop contacting you

To exercise any of these, contact support. We respond within 30 days.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Access to customer contact details is limited to staff who need it to resolve a problem. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat a data breach as an incident to be disclosed, not hidden.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top of this page and email registered accounts.
