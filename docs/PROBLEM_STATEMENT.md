# Problem statement

## The problem

Customers looking for local tradespeople and service professionals often rely
on personal referrals, WhatsApp groups, social-media posts, or informal phone
contacts. These channels can make it difficult to find professionals serving
the right area, compare experience and services, understand expected pricing,
and assess previous customer experiences.

Independent providers face the other side of the problem: they may have limited
online visibility, few structured ways to present their work, and no
consistent workflow for receiving and managing service enquiries.

## Who experiences it

- Customers who need a local repair, household, transport, learning, or other
  professional service.
- Independent service providers and small businesses seeking relevant local
  customers.
- Marketplace operators who need consistent tools to maintain account and
  listing quality.

## Why current approaches fall short

- Referrals and group posts are fragmented and difficult to search later.
- Customers may not have enough information to compare providers before
  contacting them.
- Availability, job details, and location are exchanged across disconnected
  conversations.
- Providers have limited structured feedback that can help demonstrate
  reliability.

## Proposed response

HandyNaija provides searchable service listings and provider profiles,
location-aware discovery, service requests with trackable statuses, and
post-service reviews. It gives providers a profile and service catalogue, and
gives marketplace administrators tools to monitor quality as those tools are
implemented.

## Assumptions to validate

- Customers are willing to compare provider profiles and reviews online before
  requesting a service.
- Providers can maintain accurate service, location, and availability
  information.
- A request/status workflow is more useful than discovery listings alone.
- Reviews tied to completed work can improve trust when moderation and abuse
  handling are in place.

Validate these assumptions with customers and providers before expanding the
product into payments, advanced messaging, or automated matching.
